The week in capital: 16 to 25 November 2026

What happened in funding, markets and rules this week, and what founders and investors should note. Each item links to its source.

Dry Powder Dispatch 2026-11-25 3 min read

Markets and macro

  • Investors Anthropic priced its IPO at $83, above a $70 to $78 range, and raised $81.34 billion on 18 November. The first close, per Stock Titan, was up 17.1%. Read the analysis
  • Investors 37 of 53 US IPOs of $1 billion or more this year traded below issue at the 19 November close. Stock Titan
  • Macro The Fed holds a 3.75% to 4% range after September's hike. Two members wanted another hike on 28 October. The next meeting is 8 and 9 December. Federal Reserve

Rounds

  • Founders 66 rounds raised $11.0 billion in the week of 16 to 22 November, up 38% from $7.9 billion the week before, per StartupHub. StartupHub
  • Investors Isomorphic Labs raised a $5 billion Series C led by Greenoaks and Sequoia Capital. StartupHub
  • Investors Shield AI raised $1 billion in a Series H. Genesis AI raised $500 million in a Series A for generalist robots. StartupHub
  • Founders Dreamteam, an AI-first CRM from former Freshworks leaders, raised $53 million from Together Fund, Accel and Premji Invest. StartupTalky
  • Founders Bharat Semi raised a $24 million seed for AI sensors, led by Info Edge, South Park Commons and Indus Bridge Ventures. StartupTalky

Funds

  • Investors Norwest closed Norwest 18 at $4 billion, its largest fund in 65 years, for the US, India and Israel. Funds this month
  • Investors Creandum closed Fund VIII at $750 million. redalpine closed Fund VIII at $250 million. Funds this month

Rules and cross-border

  • Cross-border The OCC finalised its GENIUS Act stablecoin rule on 21 October. It takes effect 18 January 2027. Treasury still owes the AML and sanctions rules. OCC
  • Cross-border UPI runs in nine countries, with Cambodia added in 2026. The RBI targets 20 by FY29. How money crosses borders

Regional data

  • Investors Europe raised $25 billion in Q3, its best quarter in four years, per Crunchbase. Europe
  • Founders Indian startups raised $2.2 billion in Q3 across 210 deals, with active investors down 24%. India
  • Investors MENA startups raised $1.65 billion in Q3, led by Saudi Arabia at about $1 billion. MENA

Dates to watch

  • 8 to 9 December 2026: FOMC meeting, with new economic projections. Source
  • 18 January 2027: The GENIUS Act and the OCC's final rule take effect. Source

Totals differ between data providers. Each item names its source so you can check the method. This briefing is public information, not investment advice.
