Questions readers ask

What is Dry Powder Dispatch?

Dry Powder Dispatch is a free publication that tracks startup funding rounds, fund closes and sector money flow across India, the US, Europe and beyond, with plain-English explainers on how venture capital and early-stage companies work.

Who runs it?

It is owned and edited by Komal Kedarnath G, based in Bengaluru, who works across founder’s-office operations and go-to-market strategy.

Where do the figures come from?

From public announcements, company disclosures, regulatory filings where available, and primary reporting. Every deal links to its source, and figures are recorded as reported without invented numbers.

What does “dry powder” mean?

Dry powder is capital that investors have committed to a venture or private-markets fund but that has not yet been invested. It sets the ceiling on what a fund can deploy without raising again.
