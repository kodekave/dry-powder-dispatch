About Dry Powder Dispatch

Follow the capital. Find the frontier.

Editor

Dry Powder Dispatch is owned and edited by Komal Kedarnath G (K K), based in Bengaluru, working across founder’s-office operations and go-to-market strategy.

It is a sibling publication of komalkedarnath.wiki and shares the same palette, typography, and theme state.

What is covered

Dry Powder Dispatch tracks startup funding rounds, fund closes, sector money flow, and practical explainers on how venture capital and early-stage companies operate.

Editorial standards & sources

Entries rely on public announcements, company disclosures, regulatory filings where available, and primary reporting. Figures are recorded as reported without hype or invented numbers. Any seeded sample entries carry an explicit sample flag.

Corrections & disclaimer

To flag an error or clarify a round detail, write to kmailstation@gmail.com.

Information on this site comes from public sources and does not constitute investment, legal, or financial advice.
