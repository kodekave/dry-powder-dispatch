Europe had its best quarter in four years. Four rounds made up close to 40% of it.

Crunchbase counts $25 billion for Q3. Tech.eu counts €27.8 billion. November added a $5 billion drug-discovery round and a run of defence deals.

Dry Powder Dispatch 2026-11-25 4 min read

Europe raised $25 billion in the third quarter, its strongest quarter in four years, according to Crunchbase data reported by The Next Web. That's up 77% on the $14 billion of Q3 2025. AI took 75%, or $18.8 billion, the highest share on record. Europe held about 16% of global venture funding.

Four rounds above $1 billion made up close to 40% of the total:

  • Mistral AI, a €3 billion Series D, about $3.5 billion in Crunchbase's figures and the largest venture round ever for a European company
  • Nscale, a $3.36 billion convertible note
  • Helsing, $1.8 billion
  • Quantum Systems, $1.2 billion

By country, the UK raised $7.5 billion, Germany $5 billion and France $4.8 billion. Germany and France had their best quarters since 2021 and early 2022.

Why the totals differ

Tech.eu counts €27.8 billion across 732 deals, up 32.4% on Q3 2025. It books Nscale as two rounds of about €2.95 billion and €2.59 billion, plus a €787 million financing in July. Crunchbase counts one note. Tech.eu puts the UK at almost €12 billion across 188 deals, about 43% of the total, with Germany at €4.9 billion and France at €4.0 billion. Other trackers that count equity only report far lower figures. Currency and definitions explain most of the gap, so quote the provider with the number.

November kept going

StartupHub's roundup for 16 to 22 November lists Isomorphic Labs' $5 billion Series C, led by Greenoaks and Sequoia Capital, as the biggest round of the week. It also lists Bending Spoons at $1.3 billion, Payhawk at $280 million with Goldman Sachs Growth, and Stark Future at $162 million with Highland Europe.

Earlier in the month, SyncGTM's verified list shows Fractile at $720 million for AI inference chips, with the UK Sovereign AI Fund taking part. UFORCE raised $700 million at a $6 billion valuation with Valor Equity Partners and the UK National Wealth Fund. Onodrim Industries raised $300 million to consolidate European defence suppliers, and Agile Robots raised $500 million from SoftBank at an $8.5 billion valuation.

What to watch

Public money sits in several of these rounds. You can't ignore it if you're raising in London, Paris or Munich. The UK Sovereign AI Fund and the UK National Wealth Fund both appear, and Bpifrance's defence fund joined an Arlequin AI round according to redalpine's announcement. Defence and AI infrastructure draw the largest cheques. The question for 2027 is whether the seed and Series A layer underneath keeps pace. Crunchbase, via Value Add VC, reports early-stage funding of $5.7 billion and seed of about $2 billion in Q3, both flat to down on Q2 (source).
